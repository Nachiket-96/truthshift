How we stay honest

Every fact shows its evidence, and unknown never counts as bad

Verified

Confirmed by a certifier, government or registry. Counts fully.

Claimed

Said by the company or a community database. Counts at 60%.

Unknown

Left out of the score. Lowers the confidence meter instead.

Every product gets a score and a confidence level. Below 40% known, it is flagged “Limited data”.

This is the part of the challenge we took most seriously: communicate uncertainty and do not mislead. Each fact is labelled verified, claimed or unknown. A chocolate bar with no information about labour is not marked unfair. It is marked unknown, and its confidence goes down.
