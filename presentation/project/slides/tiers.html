Buy Canadian, precisely

“Canadian” is four different things, so we show four tiers

TierWhat it means
Product of CanadaNearly all ingredients, processing and labour are Canadian
Made in CanadaMade here, but some ingredients are imported (peanuts, cocoa, coffee)
Canadian-ownedA Canadian company, possibly making the product elsewhere
ImportedMade outside Canada by a non-Canadian company

Example from our data: a honey labelled “Blend of Canada and Argentina” scores 30 on Buy Canadian, not 100.
